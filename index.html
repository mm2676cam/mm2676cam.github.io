About

I am an AI researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, working with Antonio Orvieto. With a background in physics and computer science, I investigate how optimization dynamics, learning objectives, and architectural inductive biases shape what neural networks learn—and the algorithms they develop internally.

I try to approach these questions mechanistically but holistically, drawing on theory, controlled experiments, and interpretability to understand learning from different perspectives. My broader aim is to understand and develop general, adaptive learning systems, particularly through meta- and in-context learning, diffusion models, iterative refinement and looping, and post-training methods.

I also believe that advances in AI should translate into meaningful, positive change. Biology is, to me, one of the most promising yet still underexplored areas for modern machine learning. This motivates my work on genomic language models, sequence-to-function prediction, and generative modelling of single-cell states and responses to genetic perturbations.

Research

Optimization & learning dynamics

How does optimizer update geometry change the computational strategies a model learns? I study optimizer-dependent inductive biases, memory strategies, training dynamics, and the relationship between optimization and generalization.

Diffusion, inference & adaptive learning

I investigate objective factorization and conditional inference in diffusion language models, alongside iterative refinement, looping, and semantic diffusion. Related interests include post-training methods, test-time training, and learning to adapt from context.

Machine learning for biology

I study how genomic language models can learn transferable representations of DNA and predict regulatory function, alongside generative models of single-cell expression and responses to genetic perturbations. I am interested in making these representations more predictive, transferable across biological contexts, and useful for scientific discovery.

Further interests include linear models and long-context modelling, graph neural network oversmoothing, and its connections to reservoir computing.
